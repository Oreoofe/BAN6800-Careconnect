# Regulatory Applicability Assessment (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA)

This is a design-stage applicability assessment, not legal advice and not a statement of compliance. Compliance can only be confirmed by CrescentCare's counsel and Privacy Officer against the deployed system.

| Regime | Applies to the capstone prototype? | Would apply to CrescentCare production? | Key obligations if it applies | Design response | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HIPAA** (45 CFR Parts 160, 164) | No. The prototype uses a public, anonymised Brazilian dataset; no covered entity's PHI is processed. | Yes, if CrescentCare is a covered entity (a provider that bills electronically) - scheduling and EHR data are PHI. | Privacy Rule (minimum necessary; uses for treatment/operations); Security Rule (risk analysis, access control, audit controls, transmission security); Breach Notification; business associate agreements with cloud vendors | Least-privilege roles, audit logging, pseudonymisation, encryption design, BAA dependency (Module 2 assumption A-2) | Designed; not verified |
| **GDPR** (EU 2016/679) | No (no EU data subjects). | Only if CrescentCare offers services to, or monitors, people in the EU (Art. 3). Unlikely for a US community network. | Health data is special category (Art. 9); profiling safeguards (Art. 22); DPIA for high-risk processing (Art. 35); transparency (Arts. 13-14) | Human decision on every action (outside Art. 22's "solely automated" scope); DPIA template = the algorithmic impact assessment; explanations per prediction | Design aligned; applicability to be confirmed |
| **CCPA/CPRA** (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.100 et seq.) | No. | Limited. PHI collected by a HIPAA covered entity is exempt (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.145(c)), and information de-identified under HIPAA's standard is exempt under Cal. Civ. Code 1798.146 if conditions are met. Non-PHI data (e.g. website or marketing data) of California residents could be in scope if CrescentCare meets the business thresholds. | Notice, access, deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing for in-scope data; de-identification disclosures | Keep CareConnect data inside the HIPAA boundary; no sale or sharing; document the de-identification method | Applicability to be confirmed |
| **ACA Section 1557, 45 CFR 92.210** | No. | Possibly. The rule requires covered entities to identify and mitigate discrimination risks from patient care decision support tools; it remained in force as of December 2025 (La Cava et al., 2025). Whether an outreach-prioritisation tool counts as supporting clinical decision-making is a question for counsel. | Identify tools that use protected characteristics (age, sex) as inputs; reasonable efforts to mitigate discrimination risk | Age and sex inputs documented; fairness audit and Option B mitigation; ablation without sensitive inputs | Designed |
| **Brazil LGPD** (data origin) | Noted only: the source data were published as an anonymised public dataset; no re-identification is attempted. | No. | - | Re-identification prohibited in data-use terms | - |

**What is not claimed:** that CareConnect is "HIPAA compliant", "GDPR compliant" or "CCPA compliant". The prototype implements privacy-by-design controls; legal compliance depends on CrescentCare's role, contracts, deployment and independent review.
