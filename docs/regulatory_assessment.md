# Regulatory Applicability Assessment (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA)

This is a design-stage applicability assessment, not legal advice and not a statement of compliance. Compliance can only be confirmed by CrescentCare's counsel and Privacy Officer against the deployed system.

| Regime | Applies to the capstone prototype? | Would apply to CrescentCare production? | Key obligations if it applies | Design response | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HIPAA** (45 CFR Parts 160, 164) | No. The prototype uses a public, anonymised Brazilian dataset; no covered entity's PHI is processed. | Yes, if CrescentCare is a covered entity - a health care provider "who transmits any health information in electronic form" in connection with a HIPAA standard transaction (45 C.F.R. 160.103) - scheduling and EHR data are PHI. | Privacy Rule (minimum necessary; uses for treatment/operations); Security Rule (risk analysis, access control, audit controls, transmission security); Breach Notification; business associate agreements with cloud vendors | Least-privilege roles, audit logging, pseudonymisation, encryption design, BAA dependency (Module 2 assumption A-2) | Designed; not verified |
| **GDPR** (EU 2016/679) | No (no EU data subjects). | Only if CrescentCare, not being established in the EU, offers goods or services to people in the EU or monitors their behaviour there (Art. 3(2)). Unlikely for a US community network. | Health data is special category (Art. 9(1)); right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22(1)); DPIA where processing is likely high-risk, including large-scale special-category processing (Art. 35(1), 35(3)(b)); transparency (Arts. 13-14) | Human decision on every action (outside Art. 22's "solely automated" scope); DPIA template = the algorithmic impact assessment; explanations per prediction | Design aligned; applicability to be confirmed |
| **CCPA/CPRA** (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.100 et seq.) | No. | Limited. Protected health information collected by a HIPAA covered entity or business associate, and medical information governed by the CMIA, are excluded (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.146(a)(1)); information de-identified under 45 C.F.R. 164.514 and derived from such patient information is excluded under 1798.146(a)(4), and loses the exclusion if re-identified. Non-PHI data (e.g. website or marketing data) of California residents could be in scope if CrescentCare meets the business thresholds. | Notice, access, deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing for in-scope data; de-identification disclosures | Keep CareConnect data inside the HIPAA boundary; no sale or sharing; document the de-identification method | Applicability to be confirmed |
| **ACA Section 1557, 45 CFR 92.210** | No. | Possibly. 45 C.F.R. 92.210 (in the eCFR current to 15 Oct 2026) bars discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age or disability through patient care decision support tools, and requires reasonable efforts to identify tools whose inputs measure those characteristics and to mitigate the risk. 45 C.F.R. 92.4 defines such a tool as one used "to aid clinical decision-making"; whether outreach prioritisation (a scheduling, not clinical, decision) is in scope is a question for counsel. CareConnect uses age and sex as inputs, so it would be identified under (b) if in scope. | Identify tools that use protected characteristics (age, sex) as inputs; reasonable efforts to mitigate discrimination risk | Age and sex inputs documented; fairness audit and Option B mitigation; ablation without sensitive inputs | Designed |
| **Brazil LGPD** (data origin) | Noted only: the source data were published as an anonymised public dataset; no re-identification is attempted. | No. | - | Re-identification prohibited in data-use terms | - |

**What is not claimed:** that CareConnect is "HIPAA compliant", "GDPR compliant" or "CCPA compliant". The prototype implements privacy-by-design controls; legal compliance depends on CrescentCare's role, contracts, deployment and independent review.

## Legal obligation vs ethical recommendation

| Control | Legal basis if CrescentCare runs CareConnect in production | Otherwise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security risk analysis, access control, audit logs, breach notification | HIPAA Security and Breach Notification Rules (if a covered entity) | - |
| Identify and mitigate discrimination risk of a tool using age/sex inputs | 45 C.F.R. 92.210, *if* the tool aids clinical decision-making | Ethical recommendation (Charter P2) |
| Equalized-odds limit of 0.05, Option B post-processing | None: no law sets a numeric fairness threshold | Ethical commitment (Charter P2, Ethics Committee) |
| Human decision on every outreach | GDPR Art. 22 only if GDPR applied | Ethical commitment (Charter P4) |
| Model card, public trust statement, explanation store | None specifically required | Good practice (Mitchell et al., 2019); supports HIPAA accounting and complaint handling |
| DPIA / algorithmic impact assessment | GDPR Art. 35 only if GDPR applied | Recommended governance practice |
