# CareConnect: how we use data to support your appointments

**What it does.** CareConnect estimates which booked appointments are more likely to be missed, so our care coordinators can offer help first - a reminder call, a transport voucher, or an earlier appointment.

**What it never does.** It never decides whether you get care. It never cancels, delays or double-books your appointment, never charges or penalises you, and never contacts you on its own. A member of staff decides every action.

**What it uses.** Information already in your appointment record: how far ahead the visit was booked, your past attendance, your age, whether a reminder was sent, and your neighbourhood. Your name and record number are removed before the system sees the data.

**How we check it is fair.** We test every version on how well it finds missed appointments for younger and older patients, men and women, and patients on income support. The first version found older patients' missed visits far less often than younger patients'. The flagging rule under review evens this out on average, partly by using a recorded random draw within a narrow range; in any one month the groups can still differ by more than our limit, and some small groups are too small to measure precisely. The rule under review also flags a small share of appointments regardless of their predicted risk, as a side effect of evening out the groups; we have told our ethics committee this, and an alternative rule without this effect is being considered. Our fairness standard is not yet met by any rule we have tested, so the system will not be used with patients until our ethics committee has decided how to proceed. Our project team has recommended that it is not used at all until we have a better model or enough additional evidence. The system is not currently running for patients anywhere. We publish these results, including where we fall short, and review them monthly and quarterly.

**Its limits.** It is often wrong about individuals: many people it flags would have attended anyway. It does not know the reasons behind a missed visit. It was first built on public data from another country and will be tested on our own patients before it is used.

**Your choices.** You can ask not to be included, ask why you were contacted, or raise a concern with our Patient Advocate. Concerns are reviewed by our AI Ethics Committee, which can pause the system.
